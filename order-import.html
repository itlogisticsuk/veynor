Import Settings

These settings apply to both Excel and PDF imports. The product owner is the contract customer such as Bellstone or Zoy. Retailers are delivery locations and are not created as Sofa2U billing customers.

Correct import model: company_id = Sofa2U, customer_id = product owner, retail_name = retailer/shop, delivery_* = retailer delivery address.
