Minimum Delivery Rules

Configure minimum delivery volumes, transport charges and approval rules.

Minimum delivery settings determine how deliveries below the agreed minimum volume are handled. Approval requirements and manual overrides can be configured independently for each product owner.
Minimum Delivery Charges are transport-related charges. When transport invoicing is disabled, these charges must not be added to invoices for this product owner. Operational approval settings remain available.
