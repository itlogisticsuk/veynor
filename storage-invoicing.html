Storage charging rules

  • Four calendar months of storage are free.
  • After the grace period, storage is charged at £1.20 per m³ per completed week.
  • Only free physical stock is chargeable.
  • Once an item is matched or reserved, future storage charges stop.
  • Previously invoiced storage weeks are excluded.
